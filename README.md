## What Makes BigNotes Different?

Most digital note apps either overwhelm you with complex tools or feel like writing with plastic on glass. BigNotes focuses on pure writing feel, clean organization, and staying out of your way.

### ✒️ Real Ink & Graphite Feel
- **Liquid Fountain Pen:** Flows with authentic fluid dynamics. Ink subtly bleeds into paper fibers, pools with darker edges, and naturally darkens in real time when strokes cross over each other—just like real wet ink.
- **Textured Pencil:** Mimics real graphite catching against the tooth of heavy paper. Perfect for sketching, diagrams, and quick marginalia.
- **Highlighter:** Clean, translucent strokes that sit behind your ink notes without washing them out.
- **Curated Palette:** Quick-access ink shades (Carbon Black, Deep Slate, Crimson, Royal Blue, Emerald, and Amber) plus soft highlighter colors.

### 📜 Continuous Vertical Notebook
- **Feels Like a Real Pad:** Scrolls smoothly top-to-bottom with gentle zoom. No floating in an endless void, and **zero screen rotation**—your page stays firmly locked so your wrist never spins the canvas while writing.
- **Visual Page Guides:** Subtle page breaks show you exactly where pages end, making your notes ready for clean printing and export.
- **Custom Paper:** Switch between pure white, warm parchment, or dark mode, with toggleable grid dots or squares.

### ✋ Effortless Gestures
- **Hold-to-Erase:** Keep your stylus tip held in one spot for a brief second to instantly trigger an eraser circle. Erase what you need, lift your pen, and immediately keep writing—no tool switching required.
- **Hold-to-Snap Shapes:** Draw a line, circle, ellipse, or rectangle and hold still at the end of the stroke. It cleanly snaps into sharp geometry.
- **Quick Radial Menu:** Tap the active pen to open a quick radial flyout for instant tip sizes and colors without cluttering the screen.

### ✂️ Practical Lasso Tool
Circle any section of your handwriting to:
- **Move:** Drag thoughts, paragraphs, or diagrams around your page to rearrange your notes.
- **Duplicate & Delete:** Quickly clone or clear selections.
- **Copy as Text:** Convert your handwritten cursive or print notes into plain text copied directly to your clipboard.
- **Solve Math:** Handwrite an arithmetic, fraction, or scientific equation, lasso it, and tap **Solve**. BigNotes parses your handwriting and writes the solution right on your page.

### 🔍 Offline Handwriting Search
- Search for words you wrote by hand across every notebook in your library.
- Fast, instant, and runs **100% on your device** without sending your notes to an external server.

### 📁 Clean Notebook Organization
- **Collapsible Dual Sidebars:** Keep your folders and note lists tucked away when writing, or slide them open to navigate.
- **Automatic Note Opening:** Tapping any folder immediately opens its top note so you can jump straight back into writing.
- **Images:** Insert, place, and drag reference images directly onto your pages.
- **Crisp PDF Export:** Export your entire multi-page notebook to sharp, high-resolution PDFs ready for sharing, archiving, or printing.

### 🔒 100% Offline & Private
- No accounts, no subscriptions, and no cloud logins.
- No analytics, tracking, or network requests. Your notes remain entirely on your tablet where they belong.
