## ✨ Why BigNotes?

BigNotes gives you the freedom of a physical notebook without any boundaries. Whether you are taking lecture notes, sketching out ideas, or solving math problems, everything feels fast and natural.

### 🌟 Key Highlights

- **✍️ Natural, Instant Inking:** Feels just like pen on paper. Optimized for stylus and active pens with near-zero lag and pressure sensitivity.
- **♾️ Infinite Canvas:** Never run out of space. Zoom, pan, and keep writing as far as your thoughts take you.
- **🖋️ Realistic Pens & Brushes:** Switch between a ballpoint pen, pressure-sensitive fountain pen, realistic textured pencil, and highlighters.
- **🧮 Handwritten Math Solver:** Write out math equations naturally, and BigNotes can calculate the answers for you directly on the canvas.
- **✂️ Easy Editing:** Use the lasso tool to select, move, resize, duplicate, or recolor strokes anytime.
- **🔍 Search Your Handwriting:** Find past notes quickly by searching words you handwrote.
- **📂 Simple Organization:** Organize your notebooks and pages into clean, custom folders.
- **📄 Export to PDF:** Turn your notes into clean, high-resolution multi-page PDF documents ready to share or print.
- **🔒 100% Offline & Private:** All notes are saved locally on your device. No account required, no tracking, and no cloud lock-in.
